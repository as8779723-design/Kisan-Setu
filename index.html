Direct income support for eligible farmer families.

  • Purpose: financial assistance
  • Who can benefit: eligible landholding farmers
  • Official source: pmkisan.gov.in
Crop insurance

PMFBY

Crop insurance protection against yield losses and climate-related risks.

  • Purpose: risk protection
  • Who can benefit: enrolled farmers
  • Official source: pmfby.gov.in
Irrigation

  • Purpose: irrigation support
  • Who can benefit: farmers and water-user groups

Modern Farming

Learn practical, sustainable agricultural methods that can reduce waste and improve productivity.

Water saving

Drip irrigation

Efficient water use that helps crops receive water directly at root zones.

  • Why use it: saves water
  • Advantages: lower wastage
  • Requirements: pipeline and filter
Soil care

Organic farming

Uses compost, crop residues, and biological methods to support soil health.

  • Why use it: lower chemical dependence
  • Advantages: long-term soil health
  • Requirements: composting and inputs
Data-driven

Precision agriculture

Uses field data to apply water, nutrients, and treatments more accurately.

  • Why use it: targeted care
  • Advantages: efficient use of inputs
  • Requirements: sensors and analysis
Nutrient planning

Soil testing

Helps match fertilizer use with actual soil needs.

  • Why use it: better nutrition
  • Advantages: cost control
  • Requirements: soil sample testing
Protected farming

Protected cultivation

Greenhouses and tunnels help control climate and reduce crop damage.

  • Why use it: seasonal protection
  • Advantages: better yield control
  • Requirements: structure and management
Vertical space

Vertical farming

Growing crops in layered systems for efficient space use and controlled conditions.

  • Why use it: smaller land footprint
  • Advantages: intensive production
  • Requirements: infrastructure and energy
